[h1]Coop Balance[/h1]
Makes co-op fairer: your partner no longer pays for your character's drawbacks, and the Random button stops forcing very hard characters on the team.

[h2]1. Fair materials in co-op[/h2]
[b]The problem:[/b] in co-op, Brotato does not apply each character's materials modifier to its own player. It [b]averages[/b] the modifiers of all players and then splits every material evenly. So if you play [b]Farmer[/b] (-50% materials) with a partner who has no malus, [b]both[/b] of you end up at -25%. Your partner pays half of a drawback they never chose.

[b]What the mod does:[/b] after every pickup, it tops up each player so that nobody suffers more than their own malus.
[table]
[tr][th][/th][th]Player 1 (Farmer)[/th][th]Player 2 (no malus)[/th][/tr]
[tr][td]Vanilla[/td][td]75%[/td][td]75%[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]With Coop Balance[/td][td]75% (unchanged)[/td][td][b]100%[/b][/td][/tr]
[/table]
[list]
[*]Works for every materials malus: Farmer, Streamer, Hiker, Survivor (all materials) and Explorer, Cryptid, Archaeologist, Buccaneer, Fisherman (materials dropped by enemies). Items that change materials drops are taken into account too.
[*]The player with the malus keeps the vanilla result: the mod never takes anything away.
[*]Bonuses stay shared like in vanilla (Jack's bonus still helps both players). Two Farmers stay at -50%.
[*]The extra materials also give the matching XP.
[*]No effect in solo.
[/list]
A small overlay at the top of the screen (right of the wave timer) shows the extra materials each player receives during co-op waves, e.g. "enemies  P1 +0%  P2 +33%".

[h2]2. Smarter Random character button[/h2]
The Random button no longer picks these characters (you can still pick them yourself):
[list]
[*][b]In co-op: characters that make the game much harder for both players[/b]: Jack (+175% enemy health, +35% enemy damage), Diver (+200% enemy health), Chef (+100% enemy health). Curious, Captain and Droid only add a small enemy buff, so they stay in the draw.
[*][b]In solo and co-op: characters that already beat Nightmare on the selected map[/b], so Random helps you discover new ones. With the map on Random, a character is skipped only if it beat Nightmare on every map.
[/list]
If a filter would leave no character to draw from, it is ignored for that draw.

[h2]3. Long item descriptions scroll by themselves[/h2]
Playing through a remote screen share where the mouse wheel and the cursor do not get through? A shop item description longer than its card now scrolls on its own: short pause at the top, slow scroll down, pause at the bottom, back up, and again. No input needed. The right stick still scrolls by hand.

[/list]
